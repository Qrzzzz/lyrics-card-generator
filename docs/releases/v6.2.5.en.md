# Lyrics Card Generator v6.2.5

> Status: released.

## Lightweight project signature

- `generated by lyric card generator` on exported cards is replaced by the shorter `Qrzzzz/lyrics-card-generator`, without a descriptive prefix.
- The signature drops both divider lines and becomes a single normal-width sans-serif line at weight 400, with a 26px portrait base, a 30px landscape scale base, and 0.02em letter spacing.
- `Qrzzzz` and `/lyrics-card-generator` remain visually continuous, with a restrained 72% versus 52% opacity difference that preserves hierarchy without becoming muddy after scaling.

## Footer and settings

- The portrait gap between the signature and platform or sharer details is reduced to 14px. Landscape keeps its existing accessories rendering chain with 16px spacing scaled proportionally, while the redundant auto-growth hint is removed from the layout step.
- “Show Generated Watermark” becomes “Show project signature” in the editor, with matching wording for the new-card default in app settings.
- Existing document and preference fields such as `showGeneratedWatermark` remain unchanged, so settings and historical documents need no migration.

## Download and upgrade

- Windows x64 installer: `Lyrics.Card.Generator.Setup.6.2.5.exe`.
- This release remains Setup-only; upgrades from earlier versions require no settings migration.
