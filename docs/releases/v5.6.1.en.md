# Lyrics Card Generator v5.6.1 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-07-15

v5.6.1 fixes a request-boundary regression introduced in v5.5.4 and restores NetEase Cloud Music search, search-result resolution, and multi-platform song-link parsing.

## Search and parsing restored

- NetEase Cloud Music search returns song candidates again, and selecting a result imports its metadata and lyrics.
- Spotify, Apple Music, NetEase, and QQ Music links once again populate metadata through the song parser.
- The underlying NetEase search, detail, and lyric protocols were unchanged; existing parsers and output shapes remain compatible.

## Request-boundary fix

- v5.5.4 compared the browser's `Origin` directly with Next's internal `request.url`. The desktop page loads from `127.0.0.1`, while Next normalizes the route URL to `localhost`, so the same loopback service was misclassified as cross-origin and returned 403.
- The boundary now reconstructs the externally visited origin from `Host`, `X-Forwarded-Host`, and the forwarded protocol. Missing origins, explicit cross-site requests, and real origin mismatches are still rejected, while the app marker and media-type checks remain unchanged.
- Shared protections for AI translation, local-audio parsing, and lyric fetching remain enabled.

## Verification

- Regression coverage now includes Next's internal `localhost` URL with an external `127.0.0.1` origin, missing origins, missing app markers, and explicit cross-site metadata.
- The production browser flow covers NetEase search, result import, and song-link parsing.
- Editing, importing, translation, export, and the v5.6.0 visual behavior remain unchanged.
