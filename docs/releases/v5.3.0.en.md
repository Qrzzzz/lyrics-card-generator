# Lyrics Card Generator v5.3.0 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-07-13

v5.3.0 unifies the top navigation for steps two through six, giving the editor a tighter and more consistent visual hierarchy.

## Main Updates

- Steps two through six now share the compact Stepper established by the lyrics workspace, combining the current step, the full six-step route, and common actions in one panel.
- Examples, Clear all, and Settings move into the right side of the Stepper heading row, removing step two’s separate action rail and the space-heavy app header from steps three through six.
- Steps three through six keep their existing settings panels, live preview, and export flow. The change increases usable space without changing card-design behavior.
- The custom Windows title bar now shows a small app icon to the left of the app name while preserving window controls, the drag region, and version information.
- The compact action area keeps icons, labels, focus restoration, and localized semantics, with tighter spacing at the 1000px minimum desktop width.

## Release Scope

- Step one keeps its existing app header and song-selection layout as the explicit boundary for this phase of the six-step visual unification.
- Lyrics documents, preview rendering, PNG export, settings data, and project formats are unchanged.
