# Lyrics Card Generator v6.5.4 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-09-19

v6.5.4 organizes settings into clearer groups and adds an author support page under About in the desktop app.

## Settings groups

- General settings place restoring application defaults in a separate Reset group, while history retention stays under History & storage.
- Appearance settings are grouped into theme and material, accent color, interface font, and interaction effects, preserving their existing behavior.

## Support the author

- Desktop Settings → About → Support the author offers voluntary USDT contributions via TRON (TRC20) or X Layer (USDT0).
- Switching networks updates the full receiving address and QR code together. Copying an address reports success or failure. QR codes and network icons are bundled for offline viewing.
- The support page participates in breadcrumbs and back/forward navigation. QR codes contain only the address; verify the asset and network in your wallet before transferring.
- The beginning and end of each receiving address use distinct colors in light and dark themes, preserving the complete address and original copied text.
