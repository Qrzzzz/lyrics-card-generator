# Lyrics Card Generator v6.2.1

> Status: released.

## Stackable lightweight notices

- Multiple notices no longer replace one another. New notices enter at the bottom while earlier notices remain stacked above them.
- Each notice receives an independent display time based on its tone, text length, and CJK character weight; timers pause while the page is in the background.
- When any notice expires, the remaining notices settle smoothly into place. Narrow and wide layouts cap the visible stack separately, with additional notices waiting their turn.

## Refresh motion for repeated notices

- An identical notice with the same tone refreshes its own display time in place instead of adding another row.
- During refresh, the old notice surface moves right and fades out while the new surface enters from the left, moves right, and fades in. Reduced-motion mode keeps only the opacity change.

## Long guidance integrated into editing

- The 12 non-empty logical-line limit for landscape cards is now shown inside the Lyrics step instead of as a temporary notice, and focus returns directly to the lyric input.
- The warning clears automatically once the content fits, can also be dismissed manually, and behaves consistently in Desktop and Web Lite.

## Validation

- Automated coverage now includes variable timing, repeated refreshes, independent exits, pending promotion, and background timer pauses.
- Stack order, horizontal slide-and-fade motion, landscape guidance, accessible announcements, and Desktop/Web Lite interactions are covered by regression tests.
