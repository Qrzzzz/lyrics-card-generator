# Lyrics Card Generator v5.10.2

> Status: publicly released through GitHub Releases.

This is a small toast-color update. Success, warning, and error messages now use dark backgrounds from their semantic color families with brighter matching borders and text, reducing visual glare while keeping each state easy to identify.

## Highlights

- Success toasts use a dark green background with bright green borders and text.
- Warning toasts use a dark yellow-brown background with bright yellow borders and text instead of a light yellow surface.
- Error toasts use a dark red background with bright red borders and text.
- Toast layout, motion, triggers, and product behavior are unchanged.

## Validation

All three states were visually reviewed and passed style-contract, type-checking, lint, production-build, and Web Lite regression gates. Approximate text-to-background contrast ratios are 5.23:1 for success, 9.52:1 for warning, and 5.84:1 for error.
