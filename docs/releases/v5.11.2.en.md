# Lyrics Card Generator v5.11.2

> Status: publicly released through GitHub Releases.

## Multi-line title fixes

- The former “Allow two-line title” option is now named “Allow multi-line title” in all six interface languages, and its behavior is no longer limited to two lines. When enabled, portrait lyric and instrumental cards can display titles beyond two lines; disabling it retains single-line truncation.
- Portrait lyric-card auto-height now accounts for title lines, preventing long titles from consuming lyric space or being clipped during export.
- Fixed 1:1 instrumental cards dynamically reserve height for multi-line song information and shrink the artwork when space is tight, keeping title, artist, and album metadata in bounds. The existing measured multi-line landscape layout was audited and needed no change; Desktop and Web Lite share the same behavior.
