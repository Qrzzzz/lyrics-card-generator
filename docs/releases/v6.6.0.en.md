# Lyrics Card Generator v6.6.0 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-10-04

v6.6.0 lets desktop users choose between traffic lights and a Windows-style title bar. Traffic lights remain the default, while the window button layout can be changed to suit your habits.

## Title bar styles

- Choose “Traffic lights (default)” or “Windows style” in Settings → Appearance → Title bar style. New installations and users who have not selected a style keep traffic lights.
- Windows style places minimize, maximize, and close buttons on the right. When the window is maximized, the maximize icon and tooltip change to “Windowed”.
- Changes take effect immediately, save automatically, and remain after restarting the app. Restoring application defaults returns the title bar to traffic lights.
