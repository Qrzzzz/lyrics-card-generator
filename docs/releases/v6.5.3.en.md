# Lyrics Card Generator v6.5.3 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-09-18

v6.5.3 fixes invisible Explicit badges on light backgrounds. The badge now follows the card text color in portrait and landscape previews and exports.

## Fixes

- With a white solid background and automatic text color, the Explicit badge now appears in black with the title. On dark backgrounds it remains white.
- Single-line and multiline titles share the same badge colors across both layouts. Turning Explicit off hides the badge; its shape, size and accessible label are unchanged.
