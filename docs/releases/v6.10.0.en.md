# Lyrics Card Generator v6.10.0

**2026-11-05**

Redesigns no-lyrics cards with edge-to-edge artwork, progressive bottom blur, larger titles and separate credits.

- Uses a fixed 1080×1512 canvas based on square artwork while preserving natural proportions for non-square covers.
- Blends two large blur levels and soft opacity masks near the bottom; reflects only a short edge strip to avoid hard seams and repeated artwork.
- Reuses existing solid-colour recommendations, cover swatches, custom HEX, white presets and automatic foreground contrast.
- Starts the 108px title in the clear artwork area; five CJK characters occupy about half the card width. Artist, album, sharing and project text use 60px, 44px, 36px and 32px respectively, with long-title fitting.
- Adds left and centered alignment for no-lyrics cards in typography settings; song information and credits follow the same alignment.
- Keeps song information across the artwork and transition, with sharing and project credits separately in the solid bottom area.
- Desktop and Web Lite share composition and layout. Export waits for completed backgrounds and ignores stale cover results.
- Existing portrait/landscape lyric layouts and multi-colour backgrounds retain their behavior.
