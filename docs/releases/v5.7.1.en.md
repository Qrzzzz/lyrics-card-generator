# Lyrics Card Generator v5.7.1 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-07-16

v5.7.1 continues refining the adjustable settings/preview workbench introduced in v5.7.0. It simplifies the divider and coordinates the position and size changes in the step 5→6 transition. PNG, WebP, and JPG export remain available across Desktop and Web Lite.

## Simpler resize divider

- The divider in steps 3–5 is now one full-height 1 px vertical line, with the center handle, extra border, and shadow removed.
- Hover feedback changes only the accent color; keyboard focus and dragging use the same clear color response.
- Dragging, arrow-key adjustments, Home/End jumps, double-click reset to 1:1, and assistive-technology values remain unchanged.

## Synchronized step 5→6 transition

- Step 5 keeps its current split ratio and continues sharing the same lyric-card preview instance with step 6.
- From any adjustable preview width, entering step 6 synchronizes the workbench pan with a smooth expansion to an equal half of the usable area.
- Returning to step 5 restores the previous ratio; stacked layouts below 1024 px and existing export behavior are unchanged.
