# Lyrics Card Generator v6.7.2 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-10-12.

v6.7.2 places ordinary choices in compact rows with a label on the left and a collapsed selector on the right, with refined opening and closing motion.

## Selection and interaction

- Text color, size, alignment and export choices show only the current value until clicked. A vertical list replaces the selector in place and closes after selection. Content type, layout mode and background mode remain horizontal.
- The original selector is hidden while the list is visible, avoiding a duplicated current value. Menus use the interface’s rounded corners, surface and accent colors, and adjust their opening direction near window edges.
- Opening and closing use subtle motion. Closing menus immediately stop accepting input; reduced-motion preferences switch directly between states.
- Arrow keys, Home, End, Enter and Esc are supported. Selection restores focus; clicking outside or pressing Tab closes the list. Disabled options are skipped during keyboard navigation.
- Fixes misaligned horizontal selection highlights and removes the repeated text color heading.
